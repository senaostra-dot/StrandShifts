# Strand Shifts

## Transitions Woven With Care

Strand Shifts is a Colorado-based senior transition concierge service helping older adults and their families navigate life's transitions with greater ease, clarity, and support.

From downsizing and move preparation to relocation coordination and home reset services, Strand Shifts provides thoughtful guidance every step of the way.

---

## Our Approach

Moving is rarely just about boxes.

It is about memories, routines, relationships, and creating a comfortable next chapter.

Strand Shifts blends practical coordination with compassionate support to help clients move forward feeling confident, organized, and cared for.

---

## Services

### Senior Relocation Support

Guidance through every phase of the moving process.

### Downsizing & Organizing

Thoughtful sorting, decluttering, and decision-making support.

### Move Coordination

Vendor communication, scheduling, logistics, and oversight.

### Packing & Unpacking

Hands-on assistance preparing and settling into a new home.

### Home Reset Services

Creating calm, functional, and welcoming living spaces.

### Donation & Transition Support

Helping clients responsibly distribute belongings and simplify transitions.
